# Claude Code Hands-On Course | Full Table of Contents

A complete course for building a personal AI work system with Claude Code / Claude Desktop. Starting from installation and safe usage, moving through project management, content creation, office collaboration, automation and tool integration, and delivery of results — finally consolidating all practices into a sustainable, reusable personal AI resource library.

The full course spans **8 chapters (including Chapter 0), 51 lessons**. Each lesson is an independent Markdown course blueprint containing learning objectives, suggested duration, prerequisite knowledge, a course outline, hands-on demos/exercises, FAQs, homework, and further resources.

This course corresponds to the "From Conversation to Automation: The Complete Trilogy" positioning narrative — **Part 1 The Brain (conversational brain) → Part 2 The Co-Worker (desktop collaboration) → Part 3 The Builder (terminal automation)** — and expands on this foundation into a full 8-chapter in-depth course. See the "Mapping to the 'Trilogy' Positioning" section below for details.

## How to Use This Course

- The `00-Chapter-Overview.md` file in each chapter folder is that chapter's positioning statement, lesson schedule, student prerequisites, deliverable standards, and instructor notes — **it's recommended to read the overview before reading individual lessons**;
- Lesson files are sorted by "numeric prefix" (e.g. `1.1`, `2.3`), and the filename is the lesson title;
- There is a clear asset-reuse chain running across chapters (see "Threads Running Through the Course" below) — it's recommended to study in order rather than skipping between chapters.

## Mapping to the "Trilogy" Positioning

The course's external positioning copy uses a "From Conversation to Automation: The Complete Trilogy" narrative structure (The Brain → The Co-Worker → The Builder, a condensed version of about 110 minutes). This 8-chapter course is the **fully expanded version** of that narrative. The mapping is as follows:

| Trilogy | Positioning Description | Corresponding Chapter | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Part 1 — The Brain** | Claude Sonnet brain upgrade: structured prompts, Artifacts collaboration, knowledge-extraction SOP, role-play instructions, 5 templates | **Chapter 0** | One-to-one mapping; Chapter 0's 0.1–0.5 cover Part 1's five points item by item |
| **Part 2 — The Co-Worker** | Claude Cowork desktop collaboration flow: Skills custom commands, local file integration, cross-app processing, Notion/Drive integration, collaboration SOP | **Chapter 4** | One-to-one mapping; Chapter 4's 4.1–4.10 fully cover the Cowork workflow; Skills/Plugins and "one-click trigger" are in 4.9, Notion/Google Drive integration is in 4.10, and the AI collaboration SOP checklist is in 4.8 |
| **Part 3 — The Builder** | Claude Code hands-on: zero-foundation terminal primer, installation and configuration, writing scripts in natural language, Cron, Hooks, MCP, hands-on automated daily report | **Chapter 5** (+ 1.3, 6.5) | The zero-foundation terminal primer and installation are in 1.3; scripting/MCP/Cron/Hooks are in 5.1–5.7; the hands-on automated daily report is in 5.8. **Note**: Part 3's closing promise that "you can close your laptop and the AI keeps working" **cannot** be fulfilled by Chapter 5's local-machine approach alone (5.6/5.8 both explicitly note that local cron depends on the computer being powered on) — truly delivering on this requires the cloud deployment in Chapter 6's 6.5. If marketing copy reuses this closing line, make sure students reach 6.5 before the promise is considered fully delivered |

**Trade-off recommendations for the condensed version (110-minute trilogy) vs. the full version (8 chapters):**

- For a trial/taster class, you can teach only Chapter 0 (condensed to 0.1 + 0.5, about 35 minutes) + 2–3 selected lessons from Chapter 4 + 2–3 selected lessons from Chapter 5, reproducing the trilogy's "light conversation → collaboration → automation" rhythm;
- The additional Chapters 1, 2, 3, 6, and 7 in the full course (tool installation and safety, project context management, content creation system, delivery of results, resource-library consolidation) are deepening content beyond the trilogy, helping students progress from "experiencing the trilogy" to "building a sustainably operating personal AI work system."

## Threads Running Through the Course

- **Safe usage**: The key management, permission confirmation, and principle of least privilege established in Chapter 1 run through the external-connection and automated-deployment scenarios in Chapters 4, 5, and 6.
- **Context management**: Chapter 2's `CLAUDE.md` / Projects are the direct foundation for Chapter 3's content context and the brief reuse in Chapter 6.
- **Content and visual assets**: The personal content context and visual design system produced in Chapter 3 are directly reused when building the website/sales page in Chapter 6.
- **Consolidating prompts and workflows**: Chapter 0's 0.5 five universal templates and Chapter 4's 4.8 Cowork prompt collection and AI collaboration SOP checklist are prerequisite material for Chapter 7's 7.2 personal prompt template library; Chapter 1's 1.6 custom commands and Chapter 4's 4.9 Cowork Skill packaging are the prototype for the formal Skill built in Chapter 7's 7.3.
- **From automation to delivery**: The scripts and scheduled tasks built in Chapter 5 (assembled into a complete automated daily-report system in 5.8) are deployed to the cloud in Chapter 6's 6.5, filling the gap that the local-machine approach cannot close — "not dependent on a personal device" — to achieve 24/7 execution.

## Suggested Learning Pace

| Stage | Chapters | Estimated Total Duration |
|---|---|---|
| The Conversational Brain (The Brain) | Chapter 0 | About 2.25 hours |
| Foundations | Chapters 1–2 | About 5.6 hours |
| Hands-On I: Content and Collaboration | Chapters 3–4 | About 6.2 hours |
| Hands-On II: Automation and Delivery | Chapters 5–6 | About 6.4 hours |
| Wrap-Up: Systematic Consolidation | Chapter 7 | About 2.75 hours |

The full course runs about **23–24 hours** in total. It's recommended to proceed at a pace of 1–2 chapters per week, paired with practice on real projects, rather than cramming it all at once.
