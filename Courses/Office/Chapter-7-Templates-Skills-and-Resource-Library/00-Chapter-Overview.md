# Chapter 7 | Templates, Skills, and Resource Library

## Chapter Positioning

This is the final chapter of the course, and also the "wrap-up and integration" chapter. Across the previous 6 chapters, learners accumulated a large amount of scattered but genuine hands-on output in tool usage (Chapters 1–2), content creation (Chapter 3), office collaboration (Chapter 4), automation (Chapter 5), and deliverable production (Chapter 6) — prompts, CLAUDE.md files, custom commands, scripts, deployment experience. The task of Chapter 7 is: **to systematically organize these scattered assets into a personal AI work system that is sustainably reusable, iterable, and shareable**.

By the end of this chapter, learners should be able to:

1. Understand the ecosystem of Claude Skills and open-source projects worth installing, expanding their own toolkit;
2. Build a structured personal prompt template library;
3. Package frequently used workflows into one-click Skills (an advancement on the custom commands from Chapter 1);
4. Establish a complete personal AI resource library management system;
5. Reference/build a reusable workflow template library;
6. Achieve the ultimate goal of the course — building a personal, continuously operating AI work system.

This chapter does not introduce much brand-new operational knowledge; its core is to **organize, consolidate, and systematize** the learning outcomes from the previous 6 chapters. It is the closing and culmination of the entire course.

## Learner Prerequisites

- Have completed all content in Chapters 1–6;
- It is recommended that, before class, learners gather the documents, scripts, and commands produced from the homework of the previous 6 chapters into one place (if these have already been stored according to the project structure recommended by the course, this step will be easy). This chapter will make extensive use of these existing outputs.

## Chapter Deliverables / Assessment Criteria

After completing this chapter, learners should produce:

- [ ] A personal prompt template library (organized by category, with at least 15 entries, which may draw on accumulation from previous chapters);
- [ ] At least 1 formally packaged Claude Code Skill;
- [ ] A structured personal AI resource library (including a description of the directory structure);
- [ ] A "My AI Work System" summary document that lays out standard workflows for various scenarios.

## Instructor Notes

- This chapter is an integration lesson of "reviewing the old to learn the new." Instructors should guide learners to revisit their own genuine output from the previous 6 chapters, rather than generating new practice material;
- Sections 7.1 and 7.5 involve specific lists of third-party Skills/projects/templates. Since the community ecosystem evolves quickly, the course team needs to regularly (at least quarterly is recommended) verify and update the recommended lists, to avoid recommending resources that have become broken or degraded in quality;
- Section 7.6 is recommended to be designed as a lesson with a strong sense of graduation ceremony. Consider arranging for learners to present their "AI work systems" to one another, to enhance the visibility and sense of accomplishment around their learning outcomes.
