## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: After the course ends, does this system still require ongoing time investment to maintain?**
A: Yes, but the investment will keep getting lighter — the initial setup requires concentrated effort, while later on it's just lightweight maintenance of "jotting things down as you go, periodic review." This return on investment gets better and better as the system matures.

**Q: If Claude Code's features get updated later, will what I learned in this course become outdated?**
A: The specific operational details (command names, interface locations) may change with version updates, but the methodology that runs throughout the course — clear instructions, context management, safe habits, systematic consolidation — is a durable, foundational capability. The tools will change, but the value of these ways of thinking won't become outdated.
