# 7.1 | 50 Must-Have Claude Skills and Open-Source Projects

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the current distribution of Skills and open-source project categories worth paying attention to in the Claude ecosystem;
- Establish criteria for judging "whether a Skill/project is worth installing";
- Select at least 3 resources relevant to your own actual needs to try out.

## Suggested Duration

25 minutes (case walkthrough, fast-paced coverage of cases)

## Prerequisite Knowledge

- Have completed Chapter 1, Section 1.6 (custom commands) and Chapter 5, Sections 5.2–5.4 (the MCP ecosystem), and understand the idea that "capabilities can be packaged, shared, and reused."

### III. Evaluating Whether a Skill/Open-Source Project Is Worth Installing

Give learners a reusable evaluation checklist (building on the MCP Server evaluation approach learned in 5.3, expanded here to the broader scope of Skills/projects):

- [ ] **Genuine need match**: Does it correspond to a scenario you will actually encounter repeatedly, rather than "looks cool but you won't use it";
- [ ] **Active maintenance**: Is the project/Skill continuously updated, and is community feedback healthy;
- [ ] **Source credibility**: Does it come from an official source, a well-known developer, or a community project with an established reputation;
- [ ] **Reasonable permission scope**: Does installing/using it require an excessive grant of permissions (echoing the security thread from Chapter 1);
- [ ] **Manageable learning cost**: Does getting up to speed require an unreasonable amount of extra time investment relative to the payoff.

### IV. Hands-On: Selecting and Trying Out

Give learners 10 minutes to:

1. Browse the complete list in the course resource library and select 3 Skills/projects relevant to your own actual work scenarios;
2. Score each one using this lesson's evaluation checklist;
3. Install and try the one with the highest score, actually invoke it once, and record your initial experience.

### V. Class Discussion: Would You Develop a Skill for Your Own Scenario?

Pose the question to set up Section 7.3, "Packaging Workflows into One-Click Skills": "After browsing so many Skills that others have made, is there a scenario where you feel 'there's currently no ready-made solution, but I personally need this often'?" Collect a few real scenarios from learners as candidate cases for the hands-on portion of 7.3.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will installing a lot of Skills cause conflicts or slow things down?**
A: Generally, enabling them as needed won't cause obvious conflicts, but following the principle of "install as needed, periodically clean up what you don't use" is always good practice (echoing the on-demand connection principle from 5.3), to avoid your toolkit becoming so cluttered that it actually reduces efficiency.

**Q: Are all these Skills free?**
A: It varies. Most community open-source projects are free, while individual Skills that connect to commercial services may depend on the pricing policy of that underlying service — pay attention to the documentation before installing.
