# 4.10 | Notion and Google Drive Integration Ideas

## Learning Objectives

- Building on 4.7's coverage of Feishu and Google Sheets, round out the connection approach for two other high-frequency collaboration tools — Notion and Google Drive;
- Understand the common principles underlying the different tool-connection methods, so you can generalize the approach to other platforms not covered here;
- Complete the connection configuration and an actual collaboration demo for at least one of Notion or Google Drive.

## Suggested Duration

25 minutes (in-depth hands-on)

## Prerequisites

- Have completed 4.7 (the Feishu and Google Sheets connection methodology); this lesson reuses the same connection principles and applies them to different platforms.

## Course Outline

### I. Why Notion and Google Drive Get Their Own Lesson

- 4.7 covered Feishu and Google Sheets, the two most common collaboration tools in Chinese-language office settings;
- Notion (a knowledge base/note-taking tool) and Google Drive (cloud file storage) are two other categories of tools that are equally high-frequency in personal productivity and internationally-oriented teams, and many learners' actual workflows rely on them;
- The underlying connection principles are exactly the same as in 4.7 (authorize → least privilege → test the connection → actually sync). This lesson's focus is to have learners repeat this methodology on different platforms, cementing it as a transferable skill.

### II. Authorization and Safety Reminders Before Connecting (Recap of 4.7)

- Continue to follow the principle of least privilege, prioritizing read-only or scope-limited authorization;
- Periodically check the list of connected apps, and promptly revoke connections that are no longer in use.

### III. Hands-On: Connecting Notion Collaboration

1. In Claude Desktop, find the settings entry point related to integrations/connections, and select Notion;
2. Follow the prompts to complete account authorization;
3. Test the connection:

```
> Please help me create a new page in Notion, titled "[Test Page]",
> with content being the summary of the competitive analysis report we organized in 4.6
```

4. Go to Notion to confirm whether the page was created successfully, whether the content is complete and accurate, and whether the formatting (heading levels, lists) was preserved.

### IV. Hands-On: Connecting Google Drive Collaboration

1. Complete Google account authorization and connection (if you already completed Google Sheets authorization in 4.7, you can generally reuse the same authorization, depending on the scope of permissions);
2. Test syncing a file produced in this chapter:

```
> Please upload the file categorization list we organized in 4.4
> to the "Course Output" folder in my Google Drive
```

3. Go to Google Drive to confirm whether the file synced accurately, and whether the folder structure matches expectations.

### V. Generalizing: Universal Steps for Connecting Any New Platform

Help learners summarize a transferable set of general steps, rather than memorizing the specific operations for each platform:

```
1. Confirm whether the platform has an officially supported connection method (integration entry point/Plugin/MCP Server)
2. Complete account authorization, following the principle of least privilege
3. Verify the connection works using a simple, risk-free test task
4. Actually sync a piece of real output, and check whether the formatting and content were preserved accurately
5. Periodically check the authorization list, and clean up unused connections promptly
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can Notion and Google Drive also be packaged into a Skill, like in 4.9?**
A: Absolutely — for example, "organize a competitive analysis report and automatically sync it to Notion" is a great candidate for Skill packaging. It's recommended that after this lesson, you return to the method from 4.9 and package your commonly used cross-platform sync workflows as well.

**Q: What if the collaboration tools I use aren't one of these four (Feishu/Sheets/Notion/Drive)?**
A: Following the "generalizing" section of this lesson, the connection approach is the same across any platform — the core is finding out whether that platform has an officially supported connection method; the principles and operational rhythm are entirely consistent with this lesson.
